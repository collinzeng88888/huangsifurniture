01 · Company Profile

Built around the way modern workplaces come together.

Founded in 2004, Foshan Huangsi Furniture Co., Ltd. is a professional commercial office furniture enterprise integrating product development, standardized production, global sales and one-stop project support.

From our base in Foshan—one of China’s most established furniture manufacturing centers—we bring together management, design, technical and sales teams to turn a buyer’s brief into a coordinated furniture package. Every project is considered as a complete workspace: how people focus, meet, collaborate, welcome visitors and move through the day.

Our role extends beyond supplying individual items. We help align specifications, finishes, accessories, packing and delivery priorities so that multiple categories arrive as one coherent solution.

One partner. One coordinated workplace.
A Working Showroom

Designed to be experienced, not only specified.

Our Foshan showroom brings workstations, executive offices, meeting settings, storage and lounge furniture together in real workplace scenes—helping buyers compare proportion, material, color and function before making project decisions.

03 · Quality & Supply

Quality is managed through the whole order—not inspected in at the end.

Standardized processes, material selection, technical review and experienced production management help maintain consistency from approved specification through packing.

Responsible material options

Board materials can be specified to E1 or E0 environmental grades according to project requirements and supporting documentation.

Durable finishes

Melamine and PU-painted surfaces, coordinated hardware and PVC edge banding are selected for stability, clean detailing and high-frequency office use.

Project-ready logistics

Model references, carton marks, packing formats and mixed-category loading requirements are coordinated before shipment.

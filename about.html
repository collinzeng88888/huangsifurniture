About HUANGSI

Foshan Huangsi Furniture Co., Ltd. supports commercial buyers with coordinated office furniture, OEM options and project-based export supply.

Commercial Office Furniture for Project Buyers

Based in Foshan, Guangdong, HUANGSI works with distributors, contractors, designers and corporate procurement teams. The product scope includes office workstations, executive and staff desks, office chairs, reception furniture, storage, lounge seating and acoustic workplace solutions.

Projects can be reviewed as a coordinated package rather than as isolated products. This helps buyers align dimensions, finishes, accessories, packing requirements and delivery priorities across the complete workspace.

Project Supply

Product lists and floor plans can be reviewed by category, quantity, finish and destination before quotation.

OEM / ODM Support

Available project options can include dimensions, colors, materials, cable management, accessories, carton marks and packing formats.

Export Planning

Orders can be organized around approved specifications, packing requirements and mixed-category container planning.

How We Prepare a Quotation

  1. Review the product list, floor plan or reference images.
  2. Confirm quantities, dimensions, materials, finishes and accessories.
  3. Clarify destination port, packing needs and delivery priorities.
  4. Prepare a model-based quotation and identify details that require sample approval.

Supplier Qualification

Commercial buyers may request company information, product specifications, packing details and available qualification documents during the RFQ process. Requirements vary by product and destination, so any requested test standard or certification should be identified before quotation.

Project selection

Choose between an office pod and room acoustic treatment

Begin with the problem and the floor plan. A pod creates an enclosed space for calls or focused work. Acoustic wall panels form part of a room treatment plan intended to address reflected sound. These product types serve different purposes, so they should be specified separately rather than treated as interchangeable solutions.

Compare enclosed office pod configurations

Compare the acoustic pod model collection by the required number of users, outside dimensions, doorway clearance and available floor area. Include ventilation, power connections, lighting, delivery access and assembly requirements in the inquiry. Ask for model-specific acoustic test documentation before setting a sound-isolation requirement; a product photograph does not establish an acoustic rating.

Plan wall panels around the actual room

For office acoustic wall panels, provide room dimensions, wall construction, mounting areas and finish preferences. Absorption within a room and sound isolation between spaces are different requirements. Confirm the material, installation method and relevant test documents against the project brief.

Prepare a coordinated procurement brief

Mark call areas, meeting spaces and open-plan workstations on the drawing, then identify which zones need enclosed booths and which need room treatment. Send the quantity, intended use, dimensions, destination and any applicable building or electrical requirements. Configuration, performance evidence and packing details are confirmed for the selected models.

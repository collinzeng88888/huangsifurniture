Choose the workstation arrangement before the finish

Start with the number of users in each team and whether work requires individual focus, adjacent collaboration or longer bench runs. Compare modular workstation and benching configurations with the individual models in the staff desk collection. Check the full cluster footprint, occupied chair space, storage access, doors and existing building services on the proposed layout.

Coordinate storage, privacy and acoustic requirements

Keep drawers and cabinet doors clear of work positions and circulation routes. Review filing cabinets against wall dimensions and document needs, and identify whether storage is shared or assigned to individual users. Desktop screens provide visual separation; they should not be assumed to provide a measured sound-isolation result.

For calls and focused work, compare acoustic office pod models and office acoustic wall panels with the room's actual requirements. Confirm model-specific dimensions, installation, ventilation or electrical needs, and any requested acoustic evidence before approval.

What to send an open-plan office furniture manufacturer

  • A measured floor plan showing doors, columns, floor boxes and receiving access.
  • Users per cluster, model codes, quantities and the proposed desk, screen and storage combinations.
  • Worktop, frame, upholstery and screen finish references, with samples where needed.
  • Monitor and equipment requirements, cable routes and destination electrical requirements.
  • Destination, delivery schedule, installation responsibility and requested packing information.

Ask suppliers to quote the same complete configuration. Separate desks, screens, storage, chairs and optional accessories so omissions are visible. MOQ, sample timing, production schedule and freight scope are confirmed with the selected models and order quantity; they are not fixed by the collection name.

Approve the specification before bulk production

Review the layout, component list, finish references and a model-specific sample or approval drawing. For larger projects, identify installation zones and carton marks so components can be received by room or team. The quality-control process and export packing and loading review should use the same approved furniture schedule.

Can an open-plan furniture system be expanded later?

Record the model codes, approved dimensions, finishes and connection details at handover. Send these references when requesting additional workstations; compatibility, finish availability and replacement components must be checked for the later order.

How do modular workstations differ from individual desks?

A modular arrangement coordinates repeated desk units with screens, storage and shared services. Individual desks may offer a simpler layout for separate positions. Choose between them using the required team arrangement, full footprint and component schedule, rather than the number of desks in a photograph.

Coordinate the front counter, waiting area and storage

A commercial reception area serves visitors and the people working behind the desk. Foshan Huangsi Furniture Co., Ltd. supports project inquiries for reception counters, office sofas, waiting chairs and coordinated storage. Start with a measured plan showing the entrance, staff work positions, waiting area and delivery access, then select furniture for each zone.

Build the furniture schedule by function

Reception desk

Use the reception desk specification page to define the counter footprint, staff-side storage, equipment and cable access. Provide a drawing for custom dimensions or branding requests.

Visitor seating

Compare office sofas, waiting sofa chairs and lounge chairs against the number of visitors and available space. Confirm dimensions and upholstery for each model.

Reception storage

Review filing cabinets for the staff-side work area. Allow for doors and drawers to open without blocking the entry, chair movement or access to equipment.

Check the layout before ordering reception sofas

Show the furniture footprint and occupied seating space on the room drawing. Keep entry doors, staff routes and access to the counter clear. Review building accessibility and circulation requirements with the project designer; a furniture photograph or nominal seat count cannot confirm that the arrangement fits the room.

For a specific sofa reference, the CM-SF2072 model page lists configurations and material details. Compare the selected arrangement with individual waiting chairs when the room needs smaller seating groups. Each proposed model should appear separately in the quotation.

Match finishes using approved references

Specify the counter finish, sofa upholstery, chair frame and cabinet finish as a coordinated schedule. Send physical samples or controlled references for important colors and textures. Similar screen colors are not enough to establish that materials from different furniture categories will match.

Include cleaning expectations and the intended daily use when requesting upholstery options. Ask for documentation covering the quoted material and model if the project requires a particular performance standard. Do not apply a general collection claim to every seating configuration.

What a reception area furniture supplier needs for a quotation

  • Room plan, counter position, visitor seat count and furniture model codes.
  • Quantity for each item, dimensions and requested storage or equipment access.
  • Finish and upholstery references, logo artwork if required, and sample expectations.
  • Destination, receiving route, installation responsibilities and target schedule.
  • Packing dimensions, assembly instructions and requested spare hardware or parts.

Can reception furniture be supplied as one coordinated order?

Submit the desk, seating and storage schedule together through the reception furniture supplier collection. Confirm each line item's specification, MOQ, approval process and packing. Mixed-category shipment planning depends on the selected quantities and package sizes.

How should a custom reception counter be approved?

Agree on the footprint, work height, storage, equipment locations, cable route and finish before production release. Confirm branding location and any site installation details on the drawing. Use the OEM review process and quality-control checklist to record the approved reference, then coordinate export packing and delivery access.

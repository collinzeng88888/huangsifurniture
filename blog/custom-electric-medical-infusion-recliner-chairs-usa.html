Custom Electric Medical Infusion Recliner Chairs for U.S. Clinics

Specify a powered infusion chair with custom healthcare upholstery, plug configuration and a door-delivery quotation.

For an infusion center, oncology clinic, dialysis unit or outpatient treatment area, a recliner is more than waiting-room furniture. HUANGSI supplies custom medical infusion recliner chairs from our factory for project and bulk orders, including electric reclining options for U.S. customers.

Search terms buyers use — and what to specify

U.S. buyers may search for an infusion recliner, medical infusion chair, chemotherapy chair, dialysis chair, powered medical recliner or healthcare recliner. These terms can describe different specifications. Start with the intended care setting and a written schedule rather than choosing only by product photo.

Factory customization for medical recliner projects

Our production team can develop the chair around the project brief: upholstery color, custom medical leather or healthcare-grade vinyl selection, powered reclining configuration and plug type. This is particularly useful for a clinic group, distributor program or a multi-chair treatment room where the finish needs to match an established interior palette.

Medical upholstery should be selected against the facility's own cleaning agents, infection-control process and required documentation. If a project needs a named material, performance test, electrical listing or other regulatory evidence, include it in the RFQ before sampling and production. We do not represent an unconfirmed specification as certified.

Electric medical recliner configuration for U.S. projects

A powered medical recliner chair can be a practical option for extended treatment sessions, but the electrical details must be ordered correctly. For U.S. projects, send the required voltage and plug configuration; for other export markets, HUANGSI can quote the corresponding national plug standard. Buyers should confirm the final electrical and compliance requirements with their facility and local authority before the production order is approved.

From factory order to DDP ocean delivery

As a source factory, HUANGSI coordinates customization from sample confirmation through bulk production and export packing. For qualifying U.S. orders, we can provide a quotation for DDP sea freight door-to-door. A complete quotation needs the order quantity, delivery ZIP code, delivery conditions and required arrival window. Final freight, tax treatment, timing and import obligations depend on the destination and current logistics requirements.

RFQ checklist for infusion chair buyers

  • Quantity and target delivery location
  • Electric or manual recliner function
  • Required upholstery color and healthcare material specification
  • Destination voltage and plug standard
  • Required accessories, dimensions and treatment-room layout
  • Requested documentation or testing requirements
  • Whether to quote DDP ocean delivery to the final address

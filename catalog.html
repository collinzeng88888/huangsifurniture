Good to know

A few details,
before you begin.

Is there one catalog for the entire range?

Our product ranges and options evolve. We provide a current category-specific selection based on your product interests, project and destination market.

Can I request prices with the catalog?

Yes. Include your preferred models, quantities, configurations, packing requirements and destination so the quotation can reflect your project.

Can finishes and dimensions be customized?

Options depend on the model. Tell us your preferred dimensions, materials, upholstery, colors and branding requirements for review.

What about technical and compliance documents?

Specify the drawings, samples or test reports your project requires. Availability and applicability must be confirmed for the selected model, configuration and destination.

BUYER FAQ

Before you send an RFQ.

Clear project information produces faster, more accurate quotations and fewer revisions.

Send Your Requirements →
Can you supply a complete office furniture package?+

Yes. A project package can combine workstations, desks, chairs, reception furniture, storage, meeting-room furniture and acoustic products in one coordinated order.

Do you support OEM and custom dimensions?+

Custom sizes, finishes, colors, hardware, cable management, logo application and packaging can be reviewed from drawings, floor plans or reference images.

What should I include in the RFQ?+

Provide product type, quantity, dimensions, material or finish, destination port, target delivery date and any layout, drawing or packaging requirements.

Can different furniture categories be loaded together?+

Mixed-category loading can be planned for project orders according to product dimensions, packaging method and container utilization.

How are samples and finish approvals handled?+

Sample options depend on the product and customization level. Color swatches, material samples, drawings and pre-production confirmation can be arranged as part of the approval process.

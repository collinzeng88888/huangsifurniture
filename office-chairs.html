Commercial seating selection

Specify office chairs by workplace role

Prepare a seating schedule before comparing models. Workstation users, executives, visitors and training-room attendees have different layout and adjustment requirements. List the quantity for each role and coordinate the selected chairs with desk openings, table heights and circulation space.

Task seating for shared workstations

Review the mesh office chair collection for workstation seating. Compare seat dimensions, the confirmed adjustment range, lumbar support, arm configuration, mechanism and base on a model-by-model basis. Ask for the exact specification included in the quotation instead of assuming that every mesh chair has the same adjustments.

Executive, visitor and meeting seating

Use the upholstered office chair collection to compare executive and guest configurations. Confirm whether the selected model has a swivel base or a fixed frame, and specify the upholstery composition and color reference. Check the overall width and arm height against the chosen desk or meeting table. Upholstery appearance alone does not establish leather grade or performance certification.

Additional seating requirements

For electric office chairs, request the model-specific powered functions, electrical requirements and controls. For training chairs, confirm tablet arms, mobility or stacking requirements against the actual model. Compare lounge chairs separately for reception and informal seating areas.

Information needed for a bulk quotation

Send model codes, quantities, upholstery or mesh preferences, destination and required performance documents. Confirm the approved sample, dimensions, carton format and spare-parts requirements before the order. Price, order quantity, lead time and available configurations are confirmed in the project quotation.

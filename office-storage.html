Commercial storage planning

Compare filing cabinets by size and office layout

Start with a dimensioned wall elevation and the documents or equipment to be stored. Full-height cabinets behind an executive desk and low cabinets beside a workstation need different clearances. Measure the available wall width, door swing, circulation space and delivery access before selecting a model.

Finish, hardware and installation checks

Review board composition, surface finish, glass, shelf arrangement and hardware against the selected model. Request supporting documentation for any material-grade requirement. Confirm assembly method, wall fixing requirements and access for delivery before approving the order.

Prepare a bulk storage quotation

Send cabinet model codes, quantity by size, wall dimensions, color or finish references and destination. For coordinated office furniture orders, include the desks and workstation layouts so cabinet depth, door opening and circulation can be reviewed together. Confirm packing dimensions, assembly instructions and spare hardware with the final specification.

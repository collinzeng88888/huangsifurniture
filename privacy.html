Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HUANGSI handles information you provide when making a commercial inquiry.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Information you provide

When you contact Foshan Huangsi Furniture Co., Ltd. by email, telephone or through an inquiry workflow on this website, you may provide your name, company, business email, country or region, product requirements, quantities, destination, schedule, drawings and other project details.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to understand your requirements, prepare or discuss a quotation, answer questions, coordinate samples or orders, maintain business correspondence and protect the website from misuse. We do not ask for payment-card details through the website RFQ form.

Email-based inquiries

The current RFQ workflow may open your own email application with project details prepared. The message is not sent until you review and send it. Your email provider and ours may process the message according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers or project partners only when reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, prepare an order, arrange delivery, comply with law or protect legitimate business interests. We retain business correspondence for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes and applicable record-keeping obligations.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use non-essential advertising cookies. If analytics or other optional technologies are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before those technologies are enabled.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete inquiry information where applicable, subject to legal, security and business record requirements. Do not submit confidential design information unless it is necessary for the quotation and you are authorized to share it.

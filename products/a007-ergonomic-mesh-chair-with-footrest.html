Advanced commercial ergonomic seating

Independent support that adapts from head to foot.

The A007 combines a multi-axis 5D headrest, separated back and lumbar zones, 5D armrests and a seat that slides 5 cm. A dual-rod mesh footrest and four-position recline extend support for focused work, short breaks and project environments.

01

Adaptive head and lumbar support

The 5D headrest adjusts in height, depth and angle, while the lumbar unit offers five height and three depth settings.

02

Full-range working controls

5D armrests, a 5 cm seat slide, four locking positions and a pull-out footrest create a broad adjustment range.

03

Tested commercial structure

Key components are specified against BIFMA and QB2280 test requirements, with supplier-stated cyclic, impact and static-load testing.

Multi-zone ergonomic support

Independent adjustment from headrest to footrest.

The A008 combines a multi-axis headrest, separated back and lumbar architecture, 5D armrests and a cable-control mechanism. A 50 mm seat slide and length-adjustable pull-out footrest extend the usable range for focused work and short reclining breaks.

01

Six-mode lumbar adjustment

The independent variable-track lumbar unit provides three vertical and three depth settings to tune support for different users.

02

Full working-position control

A 4D headrest, 5D armrests, seat slide, height control and tilt locking provide broad day-to-day adjustability.

03

Commercial component specification

Supplier-stated load, cycle, impact and low-temperature tests are specified for key structural components against BIFMA and QB2280 requirements.

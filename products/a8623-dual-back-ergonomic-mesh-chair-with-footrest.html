Adaptive dual-back support

Adjustable lumbar contact for changing work postures.

The A8623 pairs a five-position height-adjustable back with self-adaptive sliding lumbar support. Its 4D armrests, seat slide, synchronized tilt and concealed padded footrest extend the chair from focused work to short rest periods.

01

Five-position back height

The complete dual-section backrest moves through five settings across a 60 mm range to suit different users.

02

Quiet synchronized control

A 3.0 mm reinforced mechanism provides three-position tilt locking and fore-aft seat adjustment.

03

Concealed padded footrest

The retractable support stores beneath the seat and extends for short rest or reclining periods.

Commercial purchasing

Select acoustic pods for commercial offices

Choose an office phone booth for private calls or compare office meeting pods for a furnished meeting layout. Define the intended use first, then check the selected model against room access, usable interior dimensions, power and ventilation requirements.

Phone booths for offices and meeting room pods

For an individual office pod, specify the work surface, seat and equipment that need to fit inside. For meeting booths, provide the table and chair layout as well as the proposed number of users. Pod dimensions alone do not establish occupancy or the included furniture package.

Modular office pods: plan delivery and installation

Compare packed dimensions, product weight, assembly access and the final position. Some models list hidden casters while others require tool-assisted relocation. Confirm the relocation method, furniture, outlet standard and control options for the exact model.

Frequently asked questions

Are soundproof office pods completely silent?

The word soundproof does not establish complete silence. Request model-specific acoustic test evidence, the test method and the measured configuration. Installed speech privacy also depends on the surroundings, door seals and ventilation paths.

Can a pod size determine its number of users?

External dimensions alone do not establish occupancy. Check the usable interior, selected furniture, door access and ventilation specification against the intended use and approved layout.

What should an office phone booth RFQ include?

Provide the pod model, quantity, intended use, room layout, destination, finish references, electrical requirements, furniture configuration and delivery and assembly responsibilities.

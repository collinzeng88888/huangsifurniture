Office acoustic panel OEM specification

For office acoustic panel sourcing, define the selected construction, panel dimensions, thickness, surface finish and required cut pattern. PET, fabric-faced and wood-slat references should be compared as different configurations. Confirm the material and backing for the proposed product rather than selecting by appearance alone.

Panel schedule

Provide wall elevations, finished sizes, quantities, cut-outs and the intended fixing method. Ask the supplier to confirm panel orientation, edge treatment and installation details.

Finish approval

Record colors, texture, grain direction where applicable, and a physical reference for approval. Confirm how adjacent panels will be matched for the selected construction.

OEM identification

Specify labels, project-zone references, carton marks and instruction requirements. Branding and packaging artwork need separate confirmation in the quotation.

Acoustic panel packaging and loading

Ask for package dimensions, gross weight, panel count and protection method for the quoted construction. Match the packing plan to the finished panel sizes and delivery route, especially for larger panels or project-specific cut pieces.

  • Confirm protection for panel faces, finished edges and exposed corners.
  • Identify panels by room, wall elevation or installation sequence where required.
  • Record the handling and storage instructions supplied for the selected material.
  • Review pallet or carton options and mixed-category loading against package sizes.

Quality Control

Order checks can cover material and finish confirmation, trial assembly, functional inspection, hardware counting, packaging review and pre-shipment verification according to the approved specification.

Quotation note: Final specifications, MOQ, testing requirements, packing method and delivery plan are confirmed by model and order scope.

Compare acoustic panel quotations on the same evidence

Give each supplier the same wall schedule, material requirement and finish reference. If the project specifies acoustic or fire performance, request the relevant report and check that its product description, thickness, backing and installation configuration correspond to the quoted panel. A result for another construction does not establish the performance of the proposed order.

Ask the project designer to define the required room treatment and installation scope. Panel area alone is not a performance specification. Review the meeting-room acoustic panel buying guide alongside the open-plan office layout or reception-area plan.

What should an office acoustic panel RFQ contain?

Send the wall drawings, panel dimensions and quantity, material and thickness, finish references, cut details, mounting requirements and destination. State any required test documents, installation responsibilities and packing labels before sample approval.

GF-GZB configuration and project checks

The GF-GZB reference image shows a high back with a prominent upper cushion, light-colored upholstery, brown side detailing, padded arm supports and a five-star base with casters. These visual details distinguish it from the fixed-leg GF-H380 visitor chair and the black upholstered GF-WZ swivel chair.

For an executive desk layout, compare the overall chair width with the desk opening and check the space required behind the desk. Request the seat-height range, arm height, mechanism details and upholstery composition for the quoted GF-GZB configuration; these measurements and materials are not established by the image.

Send the model code, quantity, preferred upholstery, delivery destination and any required performance documents with your inquiry. Confirm the final dimensions, sample and packing list before approving the order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Height-adjustable desk project guidance

Can the system be configured for multiple users?

Yes. It can be planned as a single sit-stand desk, back-to-back two-person bench, four-person pod or linked workstation row.

Which parts can be customized?

Options can include work-surface size and finish, frame color, privacy screens, control type, cable management, power accessories, packaging and private labeling.

Can the standing desk frame be supplied separately?

Frame-only supply can be discussed for OEM and distributor orders. Compatibility, electrical specification and packing are confirmed before quotation.

What are the height range and load capacity?

These values depend on the selected frame and configuration. HUANGSI confirms model-specific performance data in the quotation instead of applying one specification to every option.

Can I request a sample before bulk production?

Sample and pre-production confirmation can be arranged according to the selected model, finish and project requirements.

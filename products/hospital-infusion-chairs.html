Model-based project selection

Hospital infusion chair procurement

Use the model pages to identify the requested chair before obtaining a project quotation. Send quantities by room, the destination and the facility's furniture specification. Model-specific technical data and the quoted configuration are confirmed during the inquiry.

Prepare the chair and accessory schedule

  • Specify the required positions, adjustment operation and accessories for supplier review.
  • Request overall dimensions for the supplied configuration and any extended position, plus the space needed for operation.
  • Provide upholstery, color and cleaning requirements with the requested documentation.
  • Confirm included components, assembly, instructions, spare-part references and receiving arrangements.

Shortlist by model reference

Open CF-QO-YY023 Hospital Infusion Chair and HS-S-002 Hospital Infusion Chair to review their images and send the exact model codes with your requirements. Ask for the dimensions, functions, material information and applicable documents for each quoted configuration.

Compare complete project quotations

Use the same chair schedule and accessory list for every quotation. Record what is supplied with the chair, which options are separate, the packing dimensions and the agreed installation scope. MOQ and production timing are reviewed against the model, quantity and approved configuration.

Before order approval, retain the model drawing, upholstery reference, required documents and the agreed inspection scope with the purchase order. Confirm operating instructions and the process for identifying replacement components at handover.

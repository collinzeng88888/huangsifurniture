Medical sofa bed for companion-room layouts

The HS-S-510-2 medical companion sofa lists an integrated pull-out bed. When a medical office furniture brief includes a sofa-to-bed function, plan the closed footprint, full extension and operating space as separate requirements.

Listed dimension reference: Closed: 900 W × 880 D × 930 H mm. Listed extended length: 2060 mm; listed bed length: 1900 mm; inner clear width: 740 mm. Final dimensions and functional configuration require quotation and drawing approval.

Send quantity, room layout, upholstery reference, delivery destination and the facility's required material documents. Confirm care-instruction compatibility with the specified upholstery and request evidence for any performance requirement before approval.

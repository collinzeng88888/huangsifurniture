Executive ergonomic platform

Adaptive support from focused work to full recline.

The M81A combines a height-adjustable split back and adaptive lumbar system with 4D headrest and armrest control. Its four-position reclining mechanism and retractable footrest support task work, rest and executive office use in coordinated white or black finishes.

01

Adaptive split lumbar

The separated lumbar section slides vertically and adjusts forward or backward to follow the user's lower back.

02

4D contact points

The headrest and armrests provide multi-directional positioning for different users and working postures.

03

Four-position recline

A large-angle four-position mechanism works with the retractable footrest for supported task and rest modes.

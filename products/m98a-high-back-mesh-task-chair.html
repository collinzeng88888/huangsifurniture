Streamlined commercial task seating

A clean high-back profile with practical everyday adjustment.

The M98A combines a molded PP+GF back that follows the lumbar curve with breathable nylon mesh, a resilient cushioned seat and flip-up armrests. Its space-efficient structure moves closer to the desk when the arms are raised, while the upright locking mechanism supports focused daily task work.

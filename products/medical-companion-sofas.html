Healthcare furniture purchasing

Medical sofas and companion seating for project purchasing

For a medical sofa or sofa for a medical office, start with the furniture use: reception seating, waiting-room seating or a companion rest surface. The HUANGSI collection includes different constructions; a healthcare category name does not mean every model reclines or converts into a bed.

Choose by furniture configuration

  • HS-S-510-2: Medical sofa bed for companion-room layouts.
  • HS-S-511: Medical sleeper chair: compare closed and extended dimensions.
  • HS-S-545: Manual reclining companion chair for healthcare furniture enquiries.
  • JT-SA-4069: Medical waiting-room sofa in one-, two- and three-seat sizes.
  • JT-2174: Medical sofa chair for reception and companion seating.

A medical sofa bed requires a plan showing its full extension. A medical waiting-room sofa needs a schedule for seat count and footprint. For a medical recliner sofa enquiry, confirm whether a single manual recliner or a multi-seat unit is requested before comparing quotations.

Prepare a controlled furniture RFQ

Provide the model and quantity, upholstery reference, dimensions, room plan, destination and required material documents. Request the care instructions and compatibility information for the chosen surface. Any requested certification or performance claim should be supported by documentation for the quoted configuration.

How do I compare a medical sofa, sofa bed and reclining chair?

Compare the actual construction. Fixed sofas provide seating; pull-out companion models add an extended rest surface; reclining chairs change position using their specified mechanism. Confirm the function by model rather than the general category name.

Which listed models provide a pull-out companion rest surface?

HS-S-510-2 lists a pull-out bed and HS-S-511 lists a companion sleeper-chair configuration. Use their closed and extended dimensions to compare the required layout and confirm the approved drawing.

What should a medical waiting-room sofa quotation include?

Include seat count, model code, quantity, dimensions, upholstery reference, room plan, destination and required material documentation. Confirm care instructions, packing and delivery access for the selected configuration.

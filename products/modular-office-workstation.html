Frequently asked questions

Modular workstation buying FAQ

Can this workstation be configured for different team sizes?

Yes. The images show single, paired and two-person bench references. The final layout is confirmed against the floor plan, team size and project brief.

Are privacy screens and storage available?

Desktop divider screens and integrated storage references are available. Screen style, storage configuration, lock requirements and finish are confirmed for each quotation.

Can the worktop and frame colors be customized?

Project customization may include worktop finish, frame color, screen treatment, storage finish, cable access and private-label packaging, subject to model and order quantity.

Does HUANGSI supply bulk office workstation orders?

Yes. HUANGSI supports distributors, contractors and commercial office projects with bulk quotations, packing data and loading suggestions based on the approved configuration.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Share the required quantity, workstation layout, preferred finishes, screen and storage requirements, destination market and target delivery schedule.

OEM / ODM Customization

For commercial buyers, customization usually matters more than retail-style descriptions. Available options may include custom dimensions, material selection, color matching, project packaging, private-label carton marks and accessories based on buyer drawings or specifications.

Material Options

Packaging & Loading

Packaging is confirmed against the approved product specification and shipping plan. Ask for model-specific carton dimensions, gross weight, CBM, pallet options and estimated 20GP/40HQ loading quantities with your quotation.

  • KD flat-pack cartons for desks and workstations.
  • Foam edge protection and reinforced corners for boards.
  • Stackable or nested packing for selected seating models.
  • Mixed-container loading plans for project orders.

Quality Control

Order checks can cover material and finish confirmation, trial assembly, functional inspection, hardware counting, packaging review and pre-shipment verification according to the approved specification.

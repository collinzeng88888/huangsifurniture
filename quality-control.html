Buyer questions

What to define before production.

Can one checklist cover every furniture category?+

A shared project checklist helps coordinate the order, but chairs, desks, workstations, cabinets and pods need different functional checkpoints.

When should inspection criteria be agreed?+

Before production release, so the quotation, drawing, approved reference and inspection method describe the same product.

How are custom finishes approved?+

Important colors and textures should be confirmed with a physical finish reference or approved sample rather than relying only on screen images.

Can project-specific inspection be arranged?+

Yes. Share the product list, destination market and documentation needs during the RFQ so the scope can be reviewed model by model.
